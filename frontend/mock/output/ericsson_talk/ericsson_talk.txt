So what is the problem? We store more data every year, and most of it is never opened again. Meeting recordings are the biggest part. One hour of video can be one or two gigabytes. Storage costs money and energy, and it makes it harder to find what actually matters.

The second part of the problem is that the knowledge is locked inside the video. You cannot search a recording. If you want the one slide about latency, you have to watch it all.

Here are some use cases we suggest. First, turn recorded meetings into text and slide images. Second, find duplicate documents. The same deck is often stored ten times in different folders. Third, offboarding. When someone leaves, what in their files is unique and what is a copy? Whatever you build, nothing important should be lost. Keep the knowledge, drop the weight.
